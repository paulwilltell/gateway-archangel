ARCHANGEL METHOD

A fixed authority layer.
A transparent reasoning layer.

Archangel is designed to expose how a conclusion was reached—not to impersonate revelation or become a digital priesthood.

01

Claim before conclusion

The engine extracts what is actually being asserted before retrieving verses.

02

Corpus before model

Approved biblical evidence is retrieved first. A language model may organize that evidence, but may not invent new authority.

03

Certainty is labeled

Direct text, strong inference, wisdom application, disputed interpretation, and insufficient evidence remain distinct.

04

People are not scored

The platform evaluates written claims and reasoning—not salvation, spiritual rank, sincerity, or the hidden heart.

05

Safety precedes theology

Emergency and crisis referrals cannot be suppressed, delayed, or downgraded by the model.

06

Every source keeps provenance

Text, translation, language, license, version, retrieval date, and authority class stay attached to the evidence.

THE ANALYSIS PIPELINE

What happens beneath every post

1Safety scanImmediate human danger routes first.
2Claim extractionSeparate assertions from emotion and narrative.
3Scripture retrievalKJV plus approved Hebrew/Greek witnesses.
4Context testingGenre, speaker, audience, covenant, and canonical tension.
5Structured recordNo chatbot reply—only inspectable analysis.
6Loom verificationA deterministic engine grounds every claim in the corpus; ungrounded textual support is withdrawn, with a traceable derivation.
7Training gateConsent, privacy, alignment, then human review.
CONTENT POLICY

Free to post. Four narrow exceptions.

Gateway is a free place for anyone to post. No account is required, and no viewpoint, theology, doctrine, or quality judgment is ever grounds for removal — Archangel analyzes reasoning; it does not censor. Content is refused or removed only for: explicit sexual material or solicitation, abuse or harassment directed at a person, spam, and illegal content. Testimony about abuse you suffered, confession of sin and temptation, and honest discussion of sexuality are welcome here and are not violations. Removal is done by a human; automated systems can only refuse unambiguous cases at submission or flag content for human review. Removed content is hidden, never silently deleted — the audit record remains.
