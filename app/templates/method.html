ARCHANGEL METHOD

A fixed authority layer.
A transparent reasoning layer.

Archangel is designed to expose how a conclusion was reached—not to impersonate revelation or become a digital priesthood.

01

Claim before conclusion

The engine extracts what is actually being asserted before retrieving verses.

02

Corpus before model

Approved biblical evidence is retrieved first. A language model may organize that evidence, but may not invent new authority.

03

Certainty is labeled

Direct text, strong inference, wisdom application, disputed interpretation, and insufficient evidence remain distinct.

04

People are not scored

The platform evaluates written claims and reasoning—not salvation, spiritual rank, sincerity, or the hidden heart.

05

Safety precedes theology

Emergency and crisis referrals cannot be suppressed, delayed, or downgraded by the model.

06

Every source keeps provenance

Text, translation, language, license, version, retrieval date, and authority class stay attached to the evidence.

THE CANONICAL LAYER

Which Bible this platform actually loads

Gateway's canonical layer is the 66-book Protestant canon in the King James Version (1769) — 31,102 verses, public domain. This is a commitment, not a neutral position, and you deserve to know it before you trust an analysis.

What that means in practice: a citation from the deuterocanonical or apocryphal books — Tobit, Sirach, 1–2 Maccabees, 2 Esdras and the rest — is analyzed as a claim, never attested as Scripture. Archangel will say the passage is outside the loaded corpus rather than pretend it does not exist or quietly treat it as authoritative, and Loom will refuse to ground textual support on it. Catholic, Orthodox, and Ethiopian readers hold canons this platform does not load; that is a difference in premises, not a verdict that those readers are wrong. Where the canon itself is the question under discussion, Archangel is instructed to name the dispute rather than settle it.

The same honesty applies to translation. The KJV is one rendering of one manuscript tradition, and its English is four centuries old — which is why the research layer reports what words meant in 1611 alongside what the underlying Hebrew and Greek meant when written. Analyses are only ever as good as the corpus beneath them, and the corpus is named on every record: kjv-1769-full-2026-07.

CONTENT POLICY

Free to post. Four narrow exceptions.

Gateway is a free place for anyone to post. No account is required, and no viewpoint, theology, doctrine, or quality judgment is ever grounds for removal — Archangel analyzes reasoning; it does not censor. The line is conduct, not conclusions.

Content is refused or removed only for: abuse or harassment of a person; credible threats of violence; doxxing, including any attempt to unmask an anonymous member; encouraging someone toward self-harm; grooming or sexual approach to a minor; explicit sexual material or solicitation; fraud, scams, or impersonation; spam; and content the operator is legally required to remove.

What is not a violation: testimony about abuse you suffered, confession of sin and temptation, honest discussion of sexuality, disclosing your own suicidal thoughts (that is met with Scripture and crisis resources, never a block), and any theological position however heterodox or unpopular. Removal is done by a human; automated systems only refuse unambiguous cases at submission or flag content for human review. Removed content is hidden, never silently deleted — the audit record remains.
