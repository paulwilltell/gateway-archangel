I want to work through four passages about healing from family hurt.

Ephesians 4:32 tells us to be kind and forgiving to one another, so believers are commanded to forgive those who wounded them.

Hebrews 12:15 warns against a root of bitterness, so I must not let personal resentment toward my parents take root in me.

Lamentations 3:40 says to search and try our ways and turn again to the Lord, so Scripture instructs me to examine myself rather than only blaming my parents.

Galatians 6:5 says every man shall bear his own burden, so as an adult my healing is my own responsibility rather than my parents' duty to fix.

Colossians 3:13 tells us to forbear and forgive one another, which does not require pretending the harm never happened.
